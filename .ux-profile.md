# UX Profile — AI Classroom

Personal study tool. Free-tier only. Mobile-first.

## Aesthetic reference
**Notion.** Warm paper tones, editorial type, calm spacing, block/page grammar.
The board is the one bold surface: a deep slate-green panel with chalk-style writing.
Everything around it stays quiet so the eye goes to the board.

## Accessibility target
**WCAG 2.1 AA**
- Text contrast above 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text and borders)
- 44x44px minimum touch targets
- Visible focus rings, full keyboard control
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion

## Brand tone and microcopy voice
**Warm and Humancentric.** Clear, supportive, calm, like a good tutor.
Plain language, never cheesy, never robotic.
Example: "Let's pick this up from step 3", not "Resume session".

## Icon library
**Lucide** only. One set for all product UI. No emoji as interface icons.

## Banned anti-aesthetics
- Glassmorphism and backdrop-filter blur
- Floating pastel pill badges for ordinary metadata
- Decorative gradients and neon or glowing borders
- Rainbow or multi-colour gradients on buttons
- Cartoon empty-state illustrations and mascots
- Emoji used as UI icons
- Nested scrollbars, icon bloat inside inputs
- Heavy shadows, stacked oversized rounded cards, decorative icons with no job

## Constraints
- Everything free: Gemini free tier, free TTS, Supabase free tier, free hosting
- Flag anything that could hit a free-tier limit
- Layouts must pass at 375px and 1280px

## Core behaviours
- The student can interrupt the teacher by voice or button
- The teacher can ask questions and listen for the answer
- The board writes in sync with the voice: step-by-step solving for maths, quick notes for other subjects
- A slim progress strip shows the current section
